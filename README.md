**TermuxEnhanced** - a fork of Termux aimed at improving the interface and expanding the capabilities of the application.

### Tabs
- Terminal tabs are moved from the side panel to the terminal screen.
- They can be scrolled and new can be added with horizontal swipes in the terminal window.
- You can configure the position at the top or bottom, the height as single-line or two-line.
- The history of visited directories is saved for quickly adding new sessions in these directories by a swipe down on the add tab button (up if the tab panel is at the bottom).
- On exiting the application, tabs together with their session working directories are saved and restored on the next launch.

### Extra Buttons Panel
- The editor of additional buttons is moved to settings.
- For each button, you can assign a key combination for a simple press on it and for swipes on it (up, down, left, right).
- You can configure the number of columns and rows of the extra buttons panel, and also configure the appearance.
- You can configure auto-hiding of the extra buttons panel when opening/closing the keyboard.

### Input Panel
- The input panel (for entering text by swipe and simply convenient editing before pasting into the terminal) opens by the pencil button in the lower right corner of the screen.
- The history of all texts sent from it is saved for quick restoration by a swipe up from the button.
- When typing the initial part of one of the saved texts, a context window with autocomplete options from the history is shown; by a swipe right-left on one of the suggestions, you can fill in several words from it.

### Other
- Added capabilities for backup and restore of the Termux container into a tar.gz archive, as well as backup and restore of application settings into a .zip file.
- Settings from the termux.properties file are moved to the application settings interface.
- The application is fully translated into 12 languages: Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Chinese.
- More complete application of Termux Styling color schemes on the terminal screen
